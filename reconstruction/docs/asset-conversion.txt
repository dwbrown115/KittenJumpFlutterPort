The converter checks the IPA hash against analysis/artifact-identity.json and compares all 128 app files byte-for-byte with archive members before and after conversion. It writes only inside reconstruction/assets. The original artifact and extraction are unchanged.

Images:
  85 PNGs, at original dimensions, converted into assets/images.
  84 are Apple's CgBI PNG variant: raw DEFLATE, premultiplied BGRA8.
  PNG CRCs and filtered scanline layout are validated, then color channels are reordered, alpha is unpremultiplied with nearest integer rounding, and standard zlib RGBA8 PNGs are written.
  These 84 images are noninterlaced; the inherited converter also supports Adam7 and tests every filter and pass.
  All 84 files have zero maximum channel error after re-premultiplying the converted pixels.
  butterfly.png is a standard, noninterlaced RGBA16 PNG. Its channels are rounded to RGBA8 with (sample + 128) // 257. The source 16-bit decoded hash and output 8-bit decoded hash are recorded separately; this is explicit quantization, not resampling.
  Apple ImageIO independently decodes every original and output. Exact decoded channel hashes and dimensions match the manifest for all 85 files (native little-endian RGBA16 is normalized to PNG big-endian channel order).
  Pillow independently verifies all 85 output pixel hashes and dimensions.
  Quantization limitations: colors lost in original CgBI premultiplication cannot be recovered; alpha-zero RGB becomes zero. The standard 16-bit PNG is reduced to 8-bit channels for uniform runtime decoding.

Audio:
  31 .caf effects and 3 .aif music tracks become 34 mono 44.1 kHz PCM signed 16-bit little-endian WAVs in assets/audio.
  All original audio is Apple IMA4 ADPCM. CAF packet metadata gives valid frames, priming and remainder. AIFC SSND packet count and COMM rate/type are validated.
  macOS /usr/bin/afconvert performs the conversion without changing channel count or sample rate.
  An independent Python Apple IMA4 decoder validates every output sample byte against source packet data. It preserves consecutive-packet predictor precision and removes CAF priming/remainder frames using the source metadata. All 34 PCM payload hashes match exactly.
  GameLoop.wav: 5,580,672 frames, 126.545850 seconds.
  MenuIntro.wav: 770,880 frames, 17.480272 seconds.
  MenuLoop.wav: 768,576 frames, 17.428027 seconds.
  jump00..jump72, butterfly00..butterfly02, butterfly1, butterfly2, menu00, and menu01 retain their original stems as WAV names.
  Sound selection and loop timing belong to native gameplay/menu evidence, not inferred filename ordering.

Bitmap fonts and atlases:
  assets/fonts/giant_scores.fnt, small_font.fnt, and white_font.fnt are exact byte copies. Their original MisterEarlC bitmap glyph images are converted under assets/images. No external or system font files are copied.
  No sprite atlas plist files are present. Sprite rectangles and timing are compiled into the native program.
  docs/atlas-inventory.json records all major image dimensions and the parsed three BMFont glyph maps.
  Native setupWithImage evidence from renderer analysis identifies classic_atlas, grey_kitten, red_kitten and siam_kitten cells as 85x80 pixels, 12 columns: x=85*(index%12), y=80*(index//12). Their inspection grids use that evidence; animation frame timing is deliberately not assigned here.
  Other docs/atlas-inspection PNG grids are 64-pixel coordinate references only. They must not be interpreted as frame or sprite boundaries.
  docs/assets-preview.png is an inspection contact sheet, excluded from bundled assets.
