Evidence boundary
The original ARM app was not executed. Geometry and animation claims below
come from shipped pixels, source-bound native assembly and the supplemental
Ghidra dossiers under ../analysis/recovered. Those dossiers restore returning
Objective-C call flow that the initial REA decompiler result truncated; they
are analyst-restored static analysis, not canonical REA or runtime evidence.
All converted pixels are independently verified by the asset conversion work.

Recovered presentation
- Original 320 x 480 portrait coordinates, with positive world Y upward.
  Canvas maps (x, y - cameraY) to (x, 480 - (y - cameraY)). Wider/taller screens
  preserve aspect ratio and letterbox; original assets remain proportioned.
- Native Director.init at 0x1b3c8 initializes animationInterval to 0.02 seconds.
  AppDelegate at 0x215c does not override it. Gameplay and renderer decorations
  use fixed 50 Hz steps; display refresh may differ.
- The menu composes main_menu.png over menu_bg.png. Its 1 x 960 gradient is
  positioned at native center Y=480, so only source Y=480..959 is visible.
- KittenLayer.setupWithImage at 0x331c uses 85 x 80 cells and 12 columns in all
  kitten atlases. Its child sprite is centered at local (0,20).
  Classic: jump cells 0..34 at .03 s, fall cell 35, run 36..54 at .03 s,
  sit 55..60 at .04 s, resting cell 61.
  Other kittens: jump 0..44 at .03 s, fall 39..44 looping at .04 s,
  run 45..66 at .03 s, sit 67..72 at .08 s, then resting cell 72.
  Sitting schedules licking after a one-second delay; original start-lick
  and subsequent lick frame lists are rebuilt from the same function.
- DynamicsLayer.resetWithLevel at 0x7478 forces classic_atlas.png in Classic
  mode and winter_level_atlas.png in Christmas Surprise. Other levels use
  the selected red, grey or Siamese kitten atlas.
- CloudsLayer.setFrameNo at 0xa300 computes frame rectangles from each level's
  width, height, atlas width and start offset. LevelSettings data, rather than
  visual guesses or connected alpha bounds, selects all platform crops.
  Cloud.inverted governs movement; Star Wars frame variants already carry
  their corresponding artwork. Renderer does not mirror these frames again.
- BackgroundNode.resetWithLevel/quickReset at 0x6a64/0x68a0 supplies wall/tree
  tiles, 320 x 480 with 22 pixels overlap, chosen from the first three cells.
  DynamicsLayer only installs this repeating background in levels 0 and 1.
- The platform, kitten, foreground and collectible order follows DynamicsLayer
  child z values. Ground back and ending sprites use original per-level
  positions. Ground front is present only in levels 0,5,6,7.
- Cocos2d rotation is clockwise in the displayed view. Native sprite rotation
  therefore maps to positive Canvas rotation, unlike the old OpenGL build.
- FarBackground.resetWithLevel/update at 0xf78a/0xeb20 gives background clouds
  for levels 1,2,5; Earth and Galaxy for levels 3/4; stars for levels 3,4,6,7.
  Original parallax divisors, frame regions, drifting/wrapping and opacity
  transitions are reused. Classic rainbow, sun and plane are cut from
  classic_atlas.png. Christmas moon uses rect (321,881,130,130).
- Classic comet starts at the recovered 33000-height trigger, with successive
  2500-height targets and local Y motion of 8 units per simulation step. Its
  original parent/child rotation and position transform are reproduced.
- ButterflyLayer.resetWithLevel at 0x10b88 and setColor at 0x109f8 supply
  50 x 50 butterfly cells: blue 0..5, green 6..11, red 12..17. Classic
  butterflies use eight cells at atlas Y=551. Christmas gifts use rect
  (868 + variant*40,801,40,53), matching resetButterfly at 0x7b90.
- HUD digits use original fps_font1.png cells, 10 x 24 starting at ASCII comma.
  Score sits at (10,450); counter art at (145,460), count at (160,450);
  pause art at (300,460). Finite levels retain the original progress frame
  and fill at Y=440. GameLayer reset/show-counter/tick: 0x5774,0x548c,0x5578.
- The three supplied BMFont .fnt files are parsed with original glyph rectangles,
  offsets, advances and line heights. drawOriginalText exposes these bitmap
  glyphs without substituting system typefaces. The main HUD is a LabelAtlas
  in the native binary and is kept as its original image cells.
- Original finger/tilt instruction overlays appear while grounded in tutorial
  and Endless levels. Modern keyboard/control help and accessible interactions
  are supplied by the Flutter UI owned by the supervising agent.

Deliberate port choices and remaining differences
- Dart seeded random numbers make tests repeatable; scenery placement is not
  expected to match the original BSD random sequence. Native tile recycling
  is represented with periodic copies of its three source frame selections.
- The reconstruction caps elapsed time at .25 s and advances at most eight
  simulation steps per rendered update, dropping excess backlog. Pause and
  inactive scenes clear the accumulator. This prevents a long background
  interval from jumping the player ahead unexpectedly.
- Converted images use Flutter low-quality filtering (linear sampling). Original
  Cocos texture blending/filtering may differ slightly at scaled window sizes.
- Original particle-system bursts/fireworks and some score/menu transition
  actions are not reconstructed in this renderer. Their shipped texture assets
  remain bundled. Physics, score recording and level completion work separately.
- The exact original runtime timing, particle appearance and complete end-screen
  choreography remain unverified; static analysis is not a side-by-side play test.
